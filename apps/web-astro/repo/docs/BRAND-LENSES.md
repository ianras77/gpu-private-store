# Brand lenses

All brands share deterministic chart facts and report contracts. Brand treatment may alter diction, metaphor, pacing, titles, rituals, and visual presentation; it may not alter placements, houses, aspects, dates, uncertainty, ownership, or safety policy.

| Brand | Lens | Required guardrail |
|---|---|---|
| Jupiterseek | growth, opportunity, meaning, expansion with discernment | optimistic without guru certainty |
| Saturnseer | discipline, time, boundaries, earned confidence | austere without shame |
| Saturn Leo | creative authority, visibility, craft, leadership | regal without grandiosity |
| Malefic Me | friction, shadow, desire, transformation | candid without cruelty or fatalism |
| Oracle Veil | dreams, liminality, ritual, intuition | mystical without supernatural certainty |

The Report Atlas receives the same artifact structure for every brand. Brand-specific modules and copy remain owned by `packages/brands`; cross-brand snapshot tests and runtime qualification remain release gates.
