## Peer-to-peer file synchronization between your devices

Syncthing is the drop zone for shared media on this node. In this store it mounts `/media/data` directly so synced files can land where other apps already expect them.

Recommended shared folders:

- `/media/data/podcasts`
- `/media/data/dj`
- `/media/data/web-rasies`

That makes it easy to push new bedtime-story recordings, DJ sounds, and blog posts in from your devices and have apps pick them up automatically.
